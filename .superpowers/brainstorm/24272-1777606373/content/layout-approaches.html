How should the page flow?

3 architecture approaches — same brand, same sections, different scrolling experience

A — Scroll Narrative ★ Recommended

Hero → each category flows top-to-bottom with its own section header, description, and video grid. Content reveals as you scroll. Framer Motion fade-up animations, parallax on section titles. Works beautifully on all screen sizes.

B — Full-Screen Snap Sections

Each category takes the full viewport height. Scroll snaps between sections like slides. Very dramatic, like a high-end agency showreel. Alternating text-left/video-right layouts. More cinematic but harder to browse all work quickly.

C — Sticky Sidebar Navigation

Left sidebar stays fixed with section links as you scroll. Content area shows current category's videos. Very editorial magazine feel — readers always know where they are. More complex layout, excellent for desktop, needs adaptation for mobile.

All three use your brand colors, scroll animations, and the same 4 content sections.
